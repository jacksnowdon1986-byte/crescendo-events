Who we are

ABOUT US.

Crescendo Events is an integrated and innovative company delivering exceptional performance opportunities for dance schools across the UK and internationally.

20+ years of experience

We've built a trusted reputation for creating unforgettable, enriching educational and dance school trips abroad that inspire young performers and support their artistic growth.

We work closely with principals, performers and parents to ensure every event runs smoothly. Our shows and festivals celebrate the diversity of youth dance culture and welcome dancers of all ages, backgrounds and abilities.

Our competitions and festivals are adjudicated by impartial, fully qualified and experienced professionals, with a commitment to fairness and excellence.
